State Agricultural Development Index

Why SADI?

Agricultural development depends on decisions made at state level, from land access and infrastructure to funding, extension services and market connections.

Understanding how these conditions differ can help governments and investors direct resources more effectively.

AFEI is developing SADI to assess agricultural development across Nigerian states, highlight strengths and gaps, and support learning between states.

SADI provides a clearer basis for

  • 01 Policy improvement
  • 02 Investment planning
  • 03 Accountability
Forthcoming Research

Africa’s Mechanisation Economy 2027

Making mechanisation work for agricultural businesses

Mechanisation involves more than purchasing tractors. Its value depends on affordable finance, reliable maintenance, trained operators and services that farmers can access when needed.

AFEI’s forthcoming outlook will examine the businesses, policies and delivery models shaping agricultural mechanisation, with an initial focus on Nigeria. The research aims to help policymakers, investors and industry operators identify practical opportunities to expand access and improve productivity.

Publication details will be announced as the research develops.

The outlook will explore

  • 01 Equipment supply
  • 02 Leasing and hiring services
  • 03 Local assembly
  • 04 Spare parts
  • 05 The economics of ownership
